Supply room

Rules

  1. Each team chooses 6 items from the supply room.
  2. First come, first served: every item belongs to only one team.
  3. Event cards will destroy items, but some items can help you find new ones. When your team has nothing left, the zombies get you.
  4. 🛡️ Tough items always survive their first hit. Sturdy items sometimes do.
  5. 🃏 Wildcards can make teams raid, trade, lend or steal items. But thieves can get caught!
  6. In the end, only one item survives. The team holding it is rescued!
