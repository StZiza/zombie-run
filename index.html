Emergency broadcast · Antalya Sınav College

Zombies are in the city.

09:10 You are in class at Antalya Sınav College when the news breaks. The bell will not ring today. The army will rescue survivors at Antalya Airport in 3 days.

The power is going off, the doors are locked, and the zombies are coming up the street. You have 5 minutes to grab your supplies.

Choose wisely. Your life depends on it.

Rumours on the radio

  • 🌙In the dark, they can see light from very far away.
  • 👂They hunt by sound.
  • 🍯They love the smell of sugar.
  • 🌦️Forecast: rain, storms, maybe a heat wave.
  • 🌊The bridge over the Aksu River may be damaged.
  • 🦹Some survivors have become thieves.

Rules

  1. Each team chooses 6 items from the supply room.
  2. First come, first served: every item belongs to only one team.
  3. Event cards will destroy items. When your team has nothing left, the zombies get you.
  4. In the end, only one item survives. The team holding it is rescued!

Supply room

Students see the list at stziza.github.io/zombie-run/items.html. Teams send you their item numbers: type them into each team's box, or select a team and click the items.
